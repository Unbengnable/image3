Diffusion Policy — Current Stage 2 contract canary

SCOPE
Inference: observation conditioning, action denoising, partial execution and replanning.
Not a complete replacement for paper Figure 2 or a training architecture diagram.

COUNTS AND TIMES
To = 2 observations: t − 1 and t.
Tp = 8 predicted actions: t, t + 1, ..., t + 7.
Ta = 4 executed actions: t, t + 1, t + 2, t + 3.
Remaining four predicted actions are not executed before the next query.
Next query is at environment time t + 4, using the latest observation history.
To/Tp/Ta values are illustrative; no reported experimental settings are asserted.
k is diffusion iteration; t is environment time; τ is the future-action index.
Denoising direction: K → ... → k → k − 1 → ... → 0.

LOCKED RELATIONS
RGB observation history → RGB encoder → image features → condition c_t.
Proprioception q_t → condition c_t.
RGB encoding occurs once per policy query; c_t is fixed across its denoising steps.
Gaussian action noise initializes A_t^K; noisy actions enter the denoiser.
At the expanded step, A_t^k, c_t and k are inputs to εθ.
Noise prediction εθ and current noisy A_t^k enter the scheduled update.
Scheduled update → A_t^(k−1); eventual denoised A_t^0 → predicted timeline.
First four predicted actions → environment → new observations → next policy query.
Feedback arrow terminates at observation input, not predicted actions or noise.
CNN + FiLM and Transformer + cross-attention are alternative backbones.

FORBIDDEN INTERPRETATIONS
Observations are not diffused and future images are not generated.
Conditioning does not initialize action noise or become an action trajectory.
The eight predictions are not all executed open-loop.
The two backbone options are not serial modules.
Schematic curves, action glyphs and robot scenes are not measured data.
No exact DDPM/DDIM equation is shown without the schedule coefficients.
