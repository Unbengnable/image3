Diffusion Policy inference with observation conditioning and receding-horizon execution. (a) The latest RGB observations are encoded once per policy query and combined with proprioception to form conditioning c_t, which remains fixed during denoising. (b) An entire action sequence is initialized from Gaussian noise. A shared noise predictor receives the current noisy sequence, c_t and diffusion index k; a scheduled update produces the next sequence at k − 1. The expanded step represents the repeated process from K to 0. A temporal CNN with FiLM or a Transformer with observation cross-attention can provide the noise predictor; these are alternative backbones. (c) Of eight predicted actions, only the first four are executed. New environment observations supply the latest history for another policy query at t + 4.

The horizons To = 2, Tp = 8 and Ta = 4, colored action channels, curves and robot scenes are illustrative, not measured data or reported experiment settings. t denotes environment time, k denotes diffusion iteration, and τ indexes positions within the predicted action sequence. Observations provide conditioning; they are neither diffused nor generated as future images. The unexecuted portion of the prediction is superseded by replanning. Schedule coefficients, training, causal attention masks, layer-wise FiLM and warm-start details are outside this inference overview; the figure does not replace the paper's full Figure 2.
