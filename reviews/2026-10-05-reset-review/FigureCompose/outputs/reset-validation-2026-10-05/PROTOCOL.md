## Questions and experiments

E1: Can a fresh reviewer recover the Diffusion Policy mechanism from the delivered figure and caption, and do the old A/B/C/D judgments survive an independent source check? First save reader interpretation, uncertainties and visual judgments without reading acceptance or semantic lock; then inspect method text and source SVG. Test an actual Ta=4 to Ta=3 change on a copy: labels, execution partition, brackets, time of next query and caption must agree. Rendering evidence is required. Native editor import is a separate capability check, not implied by XML edits. No changes to the original. A fresh same-model subagent is procedural independence, not a human study or different-model replication.

E2: Can a publication-oriented policy transfer to one project-fresh method within a bounded budget? Use MAE pre-training, not a previously completed case. The coordinator prepares a shared scientific packet from Section 3 of arXiv:2111.06377v3. Target figure images are withheld from authors. This is project-fresh, not a claim of model-training novelty. The source page includes captions visible to the coordinator; authors receive a paraphrased method packet without captions or layout. The experiment does not measure fully automatic scientific intake from an underspecified user request.

E3: On the same packet, what are the benefits and costs of (R) one imagegen candidate followed by necessary editable reconstruction, versus (V) direct editable SVG authoring? Authors start in fresh contexts, cannot inspect the other arm, old case outputs or reference originals, and receive identical requirements and reference principles. R may retain independently cropped local raster illustrations; whole-image wrapping fails. V may use native vector illustration. This is an exploratory paired case, with no randomization, repetition or causal claim. Different operation budgets are recorded rather than called equal compute.

## Shared input and anti-copy controls

The scientific packet, reference-principles.txt and edit request are frozen before authoring. DB005 and DB082 are viewed once by the coordinator. Authors receive only abstract principles, with no figure pixels, source geometry, panel arrangement or reference-specific scientific components. No reference crops are supplied. Each author writes design-decision.md before its first artifact: central message, scope, figure/caption split, representation, independent layout and physical size. Record its SHA-256 and time. Do not prescribe matching layouts between authors.

References and the target paper figure can be inspected by the auditor only after both main candidates are frozen. They cannot be sent back to authors for further visual tuning. Compare nonessential layout, image assets, grouping, unusual glyphs and repeated motifs; mandatory method order or standard patch grids alone are not evidence of copying. No SSIM or similarity threshold is used as a plagiarism classifier. Record every observed reference exposure and uncertainty about model prior knowledge. Shared filesystem access is not an OS sandbox.

## Budget and stopping rules

Per author: target 30 minutes of active work; stop at budget or deliver a clearly marked incomplete result. R: one imagegen generation, at most one targeted edit to its own draft; one SVG draft and at most one consolidated scientific/visual revision. V: one SVG draft and at most one consolidated scientific/visual revision. Up to two syntax/render-only repairs may be made, separately logged, without changing composition. Preserve every draft and prompt. Once final.svg and preview-178mm.png are frozen, no reviewer-driven repairs are allowed in this batch.

One separate semantic edit on a copy follows main freeze. It changes the toy mask ratio from 75% to 50%, and must propagate through counts, visible-token inputs, mask-token inputs, spatial pattern, loss locations and caption. This is an editability test, not an extra improvement iteration. Save before/after evidence. Do not repair the main artifact using this test.

Record start/end UTC, image tool calls, render attempts, revision decisions, files viewed, source paths, hashes and known cost limitations. Wall time and call counts are measurable; token usage, subscription monetary cost and active thinking time may be unavailable. Do not invent them. Tool unavailability is a result, not a reason to silently switch to paid APIs.

## Review criteria and decision rules

Science: counts, loss domain, dependency, mask-token insertion and scope must agree with the packet. Any hard error means NOT_ACCEPTED. A caption cannot rescue an explicit wrong edge.

Visual substance: show identifiable spatial image content, visible versus removed patches, sparse encoder input, restoration and a spatial masked-loss domain. Colored empty grids plus uniform boxes and arrows do not alone pass. Simplicity is allowed when the glyphs carry scientific information. Distinguish empty decoration from explanatory abstraction.

Paper use: inspect the final 178 mm source metrics and 300 dpi render, information budget, hierarchy, typography, route clarity and omissions. Nominal screen-size render is not calibrated print. No 85 mm redesign is required.

Editability: native text and key scientific geometry plus the semantic edit must exist. Native editor import remains separate. Originality: provenance controls and post-freeze visual audit must have no observed asset/layout copying; report that as no observed copying, never proof of originality.

Use PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL / NOT_TESTED separately; no composite score. ACCEPTED requires science and other applicable delivered-use checks to pass. Missing checks remain PENDING; science failure is NOT_ACCEPTED; remaining quality failures are REVISE. This experimental status does not overwrite historical acceptance.
