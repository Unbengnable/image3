MAE pre-training, 50% masking edit. The toy image contains 16 row-major spatial patches; visible positions are 1, 3, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12 and 14. Only those eight patches are embedded with encoder positional information and passed to the encoder. No mask tokens enter the encoder. Eight encoded visible representations and eight copies of a shared learned mask token restore all 16 positions; decoder positional embeddings are added before lightweight decoding. All 16 patch pixels are predicted, and MSE against the original image is evaluated only at the eight masked positions (2, 4, 5, 7, 10, 13, 15, 16), shown with amber outlines. Blue positions are visible and excluded from the loss. The sailboat, mask and blurred reconstruction are illustrations rather than measured model results. Encoder and decoder positional embeddings need not be identical. Downstream recognition, benchmarks and normalized-pixel variants are omitted.
