MAE pre-training. The image is partitioned into 16 row-major spatial patches in this toy example. A random 75% mask removes 12 patches; only positions 1, 6, 11 and 12 are embedded with encoder positional information and passed to the encoder. No mask tokens enter the encoder. Four encoded visible representations and 12 copies of a shared learned mask token are restored to the 16 spatial positions, with decoder positional embeddings added before the lightweight decoder predicts patch pixels. MSE compares predictions with the original image only at the 12 masked positions, indicated by amber outlines in the prediction and target grids. Blue positions are visible and excluded from the loss. Encoder and decoder positions need not use identical embeddings. The sailboat, sample mask and blurred reconstruction are generated illustrations rather than actual model inputs or measured predictions; perfect recovery is not implied. Downstream recognition, benchmarks and normalized-pixel variants are omitted.
