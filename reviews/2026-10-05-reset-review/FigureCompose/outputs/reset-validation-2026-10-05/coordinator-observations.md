# Coordinator observations after candidate freeze

These observations supplement the independent subagent review. They are not an additional acceptance gate and were not sent to authors for repairs.

The coordinator directly viewed both frozen main renders and both 50% edit renders. The mountain-lake/pine SVG contains recognizable authored spatial content, selected image crops and explicit loss locations. The sailboat SVG contains recognizable illustrated spatial content with editable patch selection, restored positions and loss outlines. Neither is only empty colored grids. Both still rely substantially on operator boxes and arrows; this experiment does not show that that visual grammar has been overcome for every method.

At the main setting, the observed source/selected/restored patterns agree on positions 1, 6, 11 and 12. The edit renders agree on positions 1, 3, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12 and 14; counts and the loss label change to eight. XML inspection separately confirms native text and no external image links. These structural checks alone do not establish scientific or visual quality.

The raster route's actual generated PNG was inspected before its frozen SVG. It has fewer visible output blocks than its latent label and a misplaced restoration annotation. Repairs in SVG do not make that generated PNG a scientific success. Both authors' publication typography remains small enough to need actual human/physical-size verification.

The official MAE Figure 1 was downloaded and viewed only after both main candidates were frozen; URL, timestamp and hash are in coordinator-target-exposure.json. Its flamingo image is absent from both candidates. No direct asset reuse is observed in this comparison. Familiarity with MAE in model training remains unknown, and standard sparse-encoder/decoder order is required by the science. The coordinator saw the target caption during source preparation before authoring; this exposure is disclosed in source-and-exposure.json.

All 163 preexisting output files were checked against the pre-batch baseline, with zero changed or missing files. All four shared input files retain their preregistered hashes. The batch has not changed production code, contracts or prior accepted artifacts.

The coordinator also viewed both independent-review Inkscape roundtrip exports. Selected image crops expand into overlapping full scenes and obscure the sparse-input/encoder region in both. The native text counts surviving import are insufficient evidence of usable editor compatibility. This failure was not sent to authors for repair. The scientific agreement noted above applies to the delivered CairoSVG renders and inspected source, not every renderer's output.
