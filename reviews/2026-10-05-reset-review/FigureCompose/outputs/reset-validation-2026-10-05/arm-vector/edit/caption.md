MAE pre-training with sparse encoding, edited to 50% masking. The illustrative image has sixteen non-overlapping patches numbered row-major; positions 1, 3, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12 and 14 are retained (blue outlines). Only these eight patches are embedded with their positions and passed to the encoder; mask tokens never enter the encoder. The eight encoded visible patches, together with eight copies of one learned mask token M, are restored to the original sixteen spatial positions. Decoder positional embeddings are added, and a lightweight decoder predicts pixels for all sixteen patches. Original pixels provide a separate supervision target. MSE is averaged only over masked positions 2, 4, 5, 7, 10, 13, 15 and 16, outlined in orange, and does not supervise the eight visible patches.

The image, random mask sample and differing reconstructed landscape are schematic illustrations rather than model inference, measured reconstruction quality or perfect recovery. The 4 × 4 grid is a counting example, not the model's image resolution. Encoder and decoder positional embeddings need not be identical; M is shared before positional information is added. The relative encoder and decoder footprints express qualitative asymmetry, without a numerical size or speed claim. Downstream recognition, benchmarks and normalized-pixel targets are outside this figure's scope.
